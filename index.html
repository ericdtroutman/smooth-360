Smooth 360

Adds a Smooth 360 button to Warhammer product pages. Tap it for a full-screen spin viewer with steady dragging, momentum, camera height and pinch zoom, using the photos Games Workshop already publishes.

Set up

  1. Install the free Userscripts app from the App Store and open it once.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts, turn it on, and set warhammer.com (or All Websites) to Allow.
  3. In Safari, tap the button below. Then tap the puzzle-piece icon in the address bar, choose Userscripts, and tap Install.
  4. Open any warhammer.com product with a 360° view. A gold Smooth 360 button appears bottom-left. Tap it.

Controls

Drag sidewaysTurn the model. Flick and it keeps coasting.
Drag down / upRaise or lower the camera, up to straight overhead.
Pinch or scrollZoom up to 6×.
Shift + drag, or right-dragPan while zoomed (computer).
← → ↑ ↓  SpaceStep angle or height; Space toggles auto-spin.
SharpenCrisps up fine detail once the model stops moving. Tap to turn off.

Why the official viewer feels bad

Each spin is only 12 photos per ring, one every 30°, shot from 4 camera heights. The site snaps between photos and maps your drag unevenly, so the model lurches.

Smooth 360 turns at a steady rate for every pixel you drag, lets you flick it, adds camera height and zoom, and preloads every frame so it never stalls.

Not affiliated with Games Workshop. Runs only on warhammer.com, reads the spin photos already on the page you're viewing, and sends nothing anywhere. It can't run inside the Warhammer app. If the site changes how it stores spins, the viewer will say it can't find one.
